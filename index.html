Hospital 30-Day Readmission Prediction

Predicting high-risk unplanned readmissions to target early post-discharge intervention programs.

Executive Overview

Under CMS's Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP), hospitals face financial penalties for excess 30-day readmissions. This model flags high-risk patients upon discharge, enabling care teams to deploy targeted transitional care resources efficiently.
