Key Modeling Decisions
Why PR-AUC over ROC-AUC
With ~19% positive class rate, ROC-AUC can look strong while precision at any realistic staffing threshold stays low. PR-AUC and precision@K reflect what matters operationally: how many true high-risk patients you catch within a fixed intervention capacity.
Why class weighting over resampling
Imbalance was handled via class_weight="balanced" rather than SMOTE-style oversampling, to keep predicted probabilities calibrated and trustworthy for risk-stratification rather than distorted by synthetic samples.
Why narrow targeting wins on ROI
Net savings kept rising as capacity widened from 5%→30%, but ROI peaked at the narrowest, highest-confidence tier — a real constraint given most care-management teams can't enroll every discharged patient.
Honest Limitations & Next Steps
Performance ceiling is expected. Published readmission models (including CMS's own) typically top out around 0.65–0.70 ROC-AUC — a large share of risk lives outside the EHR (housing, caregiver support, adherence), not in a modeling gap.
Before production deployment: validate on a temporal (not random) holdout, run a fairness audit across race/age/gender subgroups, and set the operating threshold jointly with the care-management team.
